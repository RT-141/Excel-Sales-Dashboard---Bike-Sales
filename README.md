# Excel-Sales-Dashboard---Bike-Sales
This is an Excel sales dashboard project that focuses on bike sales in Europe and addresses multiple factors that affect sales trends, including customer demographics, behavioral patterns, and regional sales distributions. 

**1. Project Overview**

The primary objective is to support strategic business decisions and data-driven decision-making by:

**1. Analyzing Sales Trends:** Identifying high-performing regions and demographics including car owners, homeowners, 
**2. Customer Segmentation and Profiling:** Looking at key demographics, segmenting customers by age, gender, and region to understand their behavior
**3. Sales Distributions:** Examining the data and looking at trends and patterns in the total sales volumes by region, income, and occupation
**4. Data-Driven Insights:** Proposing workable and actionable recommendations to optimize efficiency, increase sales, and enhance overall performance

**2. Datasets Summary**

The analysis is based on a bike sales dataset that contains historical transactions in North America, Europe, and the Pacific region

**Key Variables:**

ID, Marital Status, Gender, Income, Children, Education, Occupation, Homeowner, Cars,
Commute Distance, Region, Age, Age Brackets, and Purchased Bike


**3. Methodology**

**Data Preparation & Cleaning:**
- Used Excel to edit and filter raw data
- Handled missing values, removed duplicates, corrected data types to ensure data accuracy
- Created a Working Sheet section to conduct the analysis and preserve the raw data

**Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** 
- Built and used Pivot Tables for creating data visualizations
- Visualized relationships between different customer demographics

**Interactive Dashboarding:** 
- Built a comprehensive Excel sales dashboard to provide a panoramic view of the business's performance
- Created and put in dynamic filters (slicers) in the Marital Status, Region, Education, Gender, Cars, Homeowner, and Occupation   categories

**4. Dashboard Proposal**

**Key Insights:**

**Customer Segmentation:** Sales are nearly evenly split by gender. Singles lead in bike sales compared to married men and women across most categories (especially among single homeowners and non-homeowners), married male and female homeowners and non-homeowners lead in sales in Europe and the Pacific, customers with Bachelors and Graduate degree lead in sales, adults (31-54) are the leading segment in the Age Brackets

**Top Markets:** North America and the Pacific regions lead in overall sales
